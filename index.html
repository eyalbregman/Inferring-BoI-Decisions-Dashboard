Inferring Bank of Israel rate decisions from the reasoning alone

Every Bank of Israel interest-rate announcement is stripped of its headline, the decision sentence, the policy-rate figure and the dates. A model then reads only what's left — the economic reasoning — and predicts lower, maintain or raise. Each announcement is judged in a fresh, isolated context.
